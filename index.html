Operation Sindoor - Full Timeline and Details

Operation Sindoor is India’s strong military response to the deadly terror attack in Pahalgam. Here is a detailed chronological timeline and full background of the events.

May 10, 2025 20:00 : Pak military broke Ceasefire

Pakistan Broke Ceasefire, multiple drone attack in Indian Cities.

May 10, 2025 18:00 : Ceasefire took effect at 5pm

Ceasefire took effect at 5pm; top India, Pak military officials to talk again on May 12.

Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri stated: "At 15:35 hours this afternoon, Pakistan's Director General of Military Operations (DGMO) contacted his Indian counterpart. Both sides agreed to cease all firing and military operations across land, air, and sea, effective from 17:00 hours Indian Standard Time today. Orders have been issued on both sides to implement this agreement. The DGMOs will speak again on May 12 at 12:00 hours."

May 10, 2025 10:00 : India's Retaliatory Strikes

🛡️ Pakistani Air Incursions on Indian Airbases:

On May 10, 2025, following the massive drone offensive a day earlier, Pakistan escalated tensions by launching coordinated air attacks targeting Indian Air Force bases at Pathankot and Adampur in Punjab. The attack involved a combination of drones, loitering munitions, and long-range missiles.

Key Points:

  • Objective: Strike Indian air defense infrastructure in retaliation for intercepted drones on May 9.
  • Technology Used: Turkish-made drones and long-range precision-guided munitions.
  • Result: India's robust air defense systems—including the S-400, Akash defense layers—successfully neutralized the incoming threats, preventing damage to military infrastructure and civilian areas.

🔥 India’s Prompt and Targeted Retaliation

India responded with a swift and calibrated military strike targeting Pakistani airbases believed to be launch sites or command hubs for the failed attack. This included high-precision missile strikes on airbases near Islamabad (Nur Khan), Muridke, and Bahawalpur.

Clarification:

These Indian operations were not pre-planned under a specific operation like "Sindoor" but were retaliatory measures directly responding to Pakistan's attempted attack on Indian airbases earlier in the day.

Indian Objective:

  • Disable launch infrastructure.
  • Deter future strikes by demonstrating rapid and precise response capabilities.

May 9, 2025: Pakistan's Massive Drone Attack at Indian Cities

May 8, Pakistan initiated a substantial drone attack on May 9, deploying an estimated 300 to 400 drones across various regions in India. These drones, reportedly of Turkish origin, were aimed at both military installations and civilian areas.

🎯 Key Details:

  • Attack Type: Swarm drone offensive
  • Number of Drones: Approximately 300–400
  • Origin: Pakistani territory, utilizing Turkish-made drones
  • Indian Response: India's integrated air defense systems, including the S-400 missile system, successfully intercepted the majority of the drones, preventing significant damage.

🛡️ Indian Cities Targeted:

The drone attacks were widespread, affecting numerous cities across northern and western India. The targeted locations included:

  • Jammu and Kashmir: Jammu, Poonch, Uri, Baramulla
  • Punjab: Amritsar, Firozpur
  • Rajasthan: Jaisalmer, Barmer, Bikaner, Pokhran
  • Gujarat: Bhuj
May 8, 2025: India's Retaliatory Strikes on Pakistani Air Defense Systems

In the early hours of May 8, 2025, India launched Operation Trinetra, a precision drone and missile strike targeting key Pakistani air defense installations. This operation was in direct response to Pakistan's attempted missile and drone attacks on multiple Indian cities Awantipura, Srinagar, Jammu, Pathankot, Amritsar, Kapurthala, Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Adampur, Bhatinda, Chandigarh, Nal, Phalodi, Uttarlai and Bhuj during the night of May 7–8.

🎯 Key Details:

  • Objective: Neutralize Pakistani air defense systems to prevent further aerial threats to Indian territory.
  • Duration: Approximately 30 minutes.
May 7, 2025 – Launch of Operation Sindoor

In the early hours of May 7, India launched Operation Sindoor, a bold and calculated air and missile strike operation targeting 9 terrorist camps inside Pakistan and PoK.

🎯 Key Details of the Operation:

  • Duration: 23 minutes.
  • Air Assets Used: Rafale fighters with SCALP missiles, AASM Hammer bombs, and Su-30MKIs for support.
  • Targets Hit: Known LeT and JeM camps in Muridke, Bahawalpur, and several PoK towns.
  • Eliminated: Estimated 70+ terrorists were killed, including trainers and senior operatives.

India stated that the strike was a "preemptive and non-military action", intended solely to destroy terror infrastructure and not target civilians or the Pakistani military.

May 1–6, 2025 – Strategic Military Readiness

During this week, India conducted mock drills and intelligence coordination exercises in preparation for a potential military response.

  • Target Identification: Satellite surveillance and HUMINT (Human Intelligence) pinpointed nine active LeT and JeM terror camps in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (PoK).
  • Air Force Mobilization: Squadrons of Rafale jets and Su-30MKIs were moved to frontline airbases.

The armed forces were on high alert, and national security teams monitored every possible counter-escalation by Pakistan.

PM Modi Grants Operational Freedom to Armed Forces

On April 29, 2025, Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired a high-level security meeting with Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, National Security Advisor Ajit Doval, and the chiefs of the Indian Armed Forces. During this meeting, Modi granted the armed forces "complete operational freedom" to decide on the mode, targets, and timing of India's response to the Pahalgam terror attack. He expressed full confidence in the professional abilities of the armed forces and vowed to pursue the terrorists and their patrons to the "ends of the earth."

National Outrage and India’s Diplomatic Offensive

India reacted with immediate and resolute action both domestically and internationally.

🇮🇳 Domestic Measures:

  • Suspension of the Indus Waters Treaty with Pakistan.
  • Severing of diplomatic ties: Indian government expelled Pakistani diplomats.
  • Trade Ban: All imports from Pakistan were banned, and Pakistani ships were barred from Indian ports.
  • Travel Suspension: All cross-border travel including buses and trains was halted.

🌐 Global Diplomacy:

Prime Minister Narendra Modi condemned the attacks and conveyed India’s “zero tolerance for terrorism” stance to global leaders.

  • Nations like the USA, UK, France, Israel, Russia, and Saudi Arabia issued strong condemnations.
April 22, 2025 – Pahalgam Terror Attack

On the evening of April 22, 2025, a gruesome terrorist attack shook the town of Pahalgam, a scenic tourist destination in Jammu and Kashmir. Five heavily armed terrorists trained by Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) ambushed unarmed tourists in the Baisaran Valley. Eyewitnesses reported that the attackers made victims recite Islamic verses to identify non-Muslims before executing them at close range.

  • Casualties: 28 civilians were killed — 25 Indian tourists, one Nepali national, and two Kashmiris (including a Muslim pony operator who tried to resist the terrorists).
  • Weapons used: Advanced automatic rifles, including M4 carbines and AK-47s.
  • Claim of Responsibility: Initially, The Resistance Front (TRF), an LeT proxy group, claimed responsibility.
  • Revelation: Indian intelligence confirmed that at least three attackers were Pakistani nationals affiliated with Lashkar-e-Taiba(LeT), with one identified as an ex-member of Pakistan's paramilitary forces.
